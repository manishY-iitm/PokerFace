Pitch: An offline, deterministic, schema-agnostic privacy firewall for messy Indian healthcare dumps (English, Hindi Devanagari, Hinglish) that eliminates direct & hidden-channel PII (0.0% leaked), links patients across scripts without merging twins or spouses (0% false merges), and cuts voter-list re-identification risk from 99.6% to 0.4% while keeping clinical utility intact.

1. Problem & Threat Model

Hospitals merging heterogeneous exports (UTF-16 TSV, cp1252 CSV, nested JSON, FHIR R4 Bundles, WhatsApp forwards, Hindi notes) leak PII in obvious fields and hidden channels (filenames, CSV headers, JSON keys, metadata paths, Base64 attachments, FHIR text.div/contained). An automated adversary holds a population voter register (Name, Gender, DOB, PIN, City) and known facts (visit dates, rare diagnoses, length of stay [LOS]). Plain regex wrappers fail on Hindi/Hinglish names, destroy medical terms (Dr. Sharma, Parkinson's, Dolo 650, H/o HTN), and cannot stop linkage or global-shift recovery attacks.

2. 4-Pass Deterministic Architecture

Pass 1: Ingest & Infer
Sorted read-only walk, SHA-256, magic-byte & depth guard (>100), encoding validator, fuzzy + value-sniffed schema inference.
Pass 2: NLP & Linkage
Zero-width index map, schwa-aware Deva→Latin, MedicalGuard span protection, 5-pass blocking, Fellegi–Sunter + Hard MNL.
Pass 3: Privacy & Scrub
HMAC-SHA256 PSN-XXXXXX & negative shifts [-365,-31], bottom-up k=5, l=2 ladder, cluster-boosted redaction.
Pass 4: Audit & Export
Self-audit scan for secret & stray PII, UTF-8 sanitized/, sorted records.jsonl, cross-checked report.json.

3. Multilingual, FHIR Hidden-Place Detection & Medical Protection

Multilingual & Obfuscated PII (src/nlp/)

  • Unicode Index-Mapped Normalizer: Strips zero-width chars (\u200b, \ufeff), folds Cyrillic/Greek homoglyphs, converts Indic digits (०-९ → 0-9), and maps spans back to original offsets.
  • Schwa-Aware Transliteration: Maps Devanagari names (राजेश शर्मा → rajesh sharma), strips honorifics, collapses Indian phonetic variants (v↔w, ee↔i, oo↔u, ph↔f, sh↔s), and parses North/South initials (R. K. Sharma).
  • Identifiers & Canaries: Verhoeff-validated & labeled Aadhaar, ABHA (14-digit), PAN, EPIC, DL, Passport, IFSC, UPI, mobile/STD via offline phonenumbers, addresses/PINs, and EN/HI/Hinglish relation anchors (S/o, W/o, पुत्र, पत्नी).

Medical Protection & FHIR R4 (medical_guard.py, fhir_handler.py)

  • Pre-Redaction Span Guard: Protects clinicians (Dr., डॉ., Consultant), 270+ medical eponyms (Parkinson's, Foley, Ringer's, Widal), 200+ Indian drugs (Dolo, Glycomet, Pan-D), dosages (1-0-1, 500mg BD), vitals (120/80, 20/20, 15/15), SNOMED/ICD-10/LOINC codes, and H/o history (never confused with Husband of).
  • Deep FHIR R4 Scrubbing: Cleans Patient fields, text.div XHTML narratives, Reference.display, contained[], non-UUID fullUrl, extension[], photo, note[].text, and Base64 DocumentReference.attachment.data while keeping 100% reference resolution and valid R4 date syntax.

4. Schema-Agnostic Field Classifier & Fail-Safe Quarantine

Zero Hardcoding (schema_infer.py + router.py): Combines fuzzy EN/HI/Hinglish header matching (handling unseen keys like rogi_ka_naam, janam_tithi, sampark_sankhya) with row/object value-distribution sniffing (detecting PAN, Aadhaar, ABHA, phone, PIN, dates inside opaque columns like col_x). Unsupported binaries (%PDF-, PNG, JPEG, XML), empty files, mislabeled JSON-in-CSV, corrupt cp1252/UTF-8 bytes, and >100-deep JSON are quarantined ("output": null) with generic non-PII reason codes; raw bytes are never copied.

5. Constrained Cross-Script Record Linkage (src/linkage/)

  • 5-Pass Deterministic Blocking (O(N·B)): Blocks on (1) Gov IDs/MRNs, (2) 10-digit phones, (3) (DOB, gender) + DD/MM swap, (4) (phonetic_last, first_initial, gender), and (5) (phonetic_first, age_5yr, gender), capping pathological placeholder blocks (9999999999) to bound runtime & memory.
  • Fellegi–Sunter Scorer + Hard Must-Not-Link (MNL): Immediately blocks merging on gender conflict, age difference >3 yrs, non-swapped DOB conflict, distinct verified Gov IDs (unless 1-char typo + strong name/DOB/phone match), or incompatible given names (preventing spouses and twins sharing phones/addresses from ever merging), while linking R. K. Sharma ↔ Rajesh Kumar Sharma ↔ राजेश शर्मा.
  • Conflict-Free Clustering & Ambiguity Holdout: Agglomerative merges require zero MNL conflicts across all member pairs (stopping A–B–C transitive chaining). Weak records lacking verified Gov IDs/MRNs that tie across conflicting clusters within ambiguity_margin=1.5 stay unlinked ("patient": null).
  • Cluster-Boosted Redaction: Linked cluster tokens (EN/HI names, relatives, MRNs) feed back into Pass 3 free-text scrubbing while strictly honoring MedicalGuard (preserving Dr. A. K. Sharma even when the patient's surname is Sharma).

6. Privacy Guarantees, Security & Utility Preservation (src/crypto.py, src/privacy/)

Keyed Crypto & Temporal Integrity

  • make_pseudonym: HMAC-SHA256(RAKSHA_SECRET, cluster_key) → collision-checked PSN-XXXXXX, embedded inside every linked record.
  • make_date_shift: Per-cluster negative shift in [-365, -31] (defeats Attack B global-shift recovery while preserving 100% exact LOS and inter-visit intervals).
  • DOB is converted to a truthful age band before shifting (never shifted into a fake birth date).

Adaptive k-Anonymity (k=5) & l-Diversity (l=2)

  • Iterative bottom-up generalization along Age (5 → 10 → 20 → *) and Geo PIN hierarchy (PIN-3 → PIN-2 → State → Region → * via indian_pin_hierarchy.json).
  • Rare-disease & elderly (≥85) outliers generalize automatically; extreme outliers suppress QIs without altering clinical values or CSV row indices.
  • Self-Audit & Security: In-memory processing only; audit.py scans all outputs for RAKSHA_SECRET and stray direct PII before exit.

8. Model Declaration & Honest Limitations

Declared Stack (Zero LLMs / Zero Transformers >50M): Python 3.10+ stdlib, rapidfuzz, regex, phonenumbers, optional PyYAML, and bundled offline gazetteers in data/. Limitations: Binary PDFs/images and HL7 XML are safely quarantined without OCR; ambiguous standalone notes whose initials and shared family phone tie across identical twins stay unlinked (null) to guarantee 0% false merges.
